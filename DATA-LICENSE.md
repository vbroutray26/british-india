# Data licence

Derived data in `data/curated/` (places, facts, census records, annotations,
translations, glossary) is released under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International (CC BY 4.0)** — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Suggested attribution: *British India: Places in the colonial record,
1860s–1947* (dataset), with the version tag and URL.

## Excluded

- **Third-party media** (photographs, engravings, maps, audio, scans) listed in
  `data/media.yaml` are **not** covered by this licence. Each keeps the rights
  of its holder; see its `credit_line`, `licence_stated` and `rights_basis`.
- **Source texts** that are in the public domain keep that status. Quotations
  are reproduced as evidence, not relicensed.

See `/credits` on the site for the full list (spec FR-MEDIA-06, FR-DATA-03).
